# New Relics!
1. Commercial Titanic Reworked:
Old:
At the start of combat, add a Manipulate Distance to your Hand and gain 4 Guided Spirits. At the start of your third turn, add a Spider Lily to your Hand. Whenever a non-minion enemy dies, gain 4 Gold.

New:
At the start of combat, add a Manipulate Distance to your Hand. At the start of your third turn, add a Spider Lily to your Hand. Whenever you Displace, gain 1 Guided Spirit.

2. Shinigami Eyes changed:
Rarity changed to Rare.
Vulnerable application increased to 3.

3. Spirit Companion. Common.
At the start of each combat, gain 5 Guided Spirits.

3. Ferryman's Oar. Uncommon.
At the start of each combat, a random card in your draw pile gains Replenish.

4. Charon's 4-Mon. Uncommon. 
On pickup, gain 44 gold. When an enemy dies, gain 4 Guided Spirits, then if it was not a minion, gain 4 Gold.

5. Parrying Scythe. Rare.
The first time each combat, before you would be attacked by an enemy whose Distance is 2 or less, gain Block equal to half that attack's damage.

6. Death note. Rare.
Whenever you exhaust, apply 2 Vengeful Spirits on ALL enemies.

7. Possessed Lily. Rare.
Whenever you are poisoned, gain Divine spirits equal to the amount of poison gained.

8. Yama's Lecture Book. Shop Relic.
The first time you release each turn, gain Spirits equal to half the amount you would release (Rounded up).

# Card Changes
1. Spirit Invitation Reworked:
Old:
Gain 3 (4) Guided Spirits and 3 (4) Divine Spirits.
Whenever you would Release:
- Deal damage to ALL enemies equal to the Guided Spirits you would Release.
- Barrier and gain Block equal to the Divine Spirits you would Release.

New:
Gain 2 (3) Guided Spirits and 2 (3) Divine Spirits.
Whenever you would Release:
- Barrier equal to the amount of Spirits you would Release, then deal damage to ALL enemies equal to your Divine Spirits' current HP.

2. Bound Spirits of the Earth:
Old:
Apply 3 (4) Vengeful Spirits to ALL enemies.
At the end of your turn, apply 3 (4) Vengeful Spirits to ALL enemies that have Vengeful Spirits.

New:
Apply 9 (12) Vengeful Spirits to ALL enemies.
Change the duration of Vengeful Spirits on ALL enemies to 1.

3. Scythe of Exorcism changed:
Old:
Deal 14 (18) damage.
Optional: Detonate, then if 4 or more Spirits were detonated, add a Spider Lily to the hand.
Release 4: Apply 4 Vengeful Spirits before detonation. Gain 1 Artifact.

New:
Deal 14 (18) damage.
Detonate, then if 4 or more Spirits were detonated, add a Spider Lily+ to the hand.

4. Handy Retreat changed:
Block increased from 6 (8) to 7 (9)
Can no longer choose to not displace.

5. Spirit Protection changed:
Old:
Gain 9 (12) Block.
Release ALL: Barrier equal to the amount released.

New:
Gain 8 (10) Block.
Your Divine Spirit heals by 3 (5).
Release ALL: Barrier equal to the amount released.

6. Eye of the Storm changed;
Old:
Gain 14 (16) Block.
Gain 6 (8) Guided Spirits.
Apply 4 (6) Vengeful Spirits to ALL enemies.
Release 6: Barrier 6 (8)

New:
Gain 10 (13) Block.
Barrier 5 (7)
Gain 5 (7) Guided Spirits.
Apply 3 (5) Vengeful Spirits to ALL enemies.

7. Tip of the Reaper changed:
cost reduced from 1->0
Damage reduced from 10 (13)->6(9)

8. Pushback Draw changed
Can no longer choose to not displace.

9. Spiritual Strike debuffed:
Damage changed from 6 (8) to 5 (7)

10. Work Focus:
Upgrade no longer lets you exhaust from the discard pile.

11. Slice of Higan debuffed:
Damage reduced from 4x2 to 3x2.
